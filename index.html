THE PROPDATA OPERATING PRINCIPLE
01 · IDENTITY

Preserve what the property is called.

Parcel IDs, UPRNs, REFCATs, titles, cadastral units and official addresses remain source-native underneath the global contract.

02 · RELATIONSHIPS

Connect the property around it.

Parcels, buildings, titles, transactions and geometries become deterministic relationships instead of disconnected datasets.

03 · EVIDENCE

Carry provenance with the fact.

Authority, producer, source system, revision and coverage state stay attached so downstream software can reason about evidence.

04 · TRUTH

Unknown stays unknown.

When an authority does not publish a fact, PropData returns a truthful null instead of manufacturing confidence.
